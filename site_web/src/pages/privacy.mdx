qot (Quantum of Thought) is made by Abstract Syntax LLC. This policy covers the qot apps (the
terminal client, the web app at app.quantumofthought.com and, when it ships, the iPhone app),
the sync server behind them, and this website. The
[Abstract Syntax website's policy](https://www.abstractsyntax.llc/privacy) covers that site only.

## The short version

- Your notes are saved on your devices. If you sign in, they are also kept on our server so
  they can reach your other devices.
- **Notes are not end-to-end encrypted yet.** They are encrypted on the way to the server, but the
  server could read them. We don't read them, and nothing we
  run does. End-to-end encryption, after which the server can't read them at all, is planned.
- We keep your email address to sign you in. No advertising, no tracking, no selling of anything.
- You can export everything and delete your account at any time ([Support](/support)).

## What's on your devices

Every note you take is saved on the device first: in the terminal, in qot's data folder; in the
web app, in your browser's storage for app.quantumofthought.com. Signing out leaves the notes
there. Deleting the app, the data folder or the site's data in your browser removes them from
that device.

You can use qot without an account. Notes then never leave the device.

## What the server stores

Only if you sign in:

- **Your email address**, which is your account. We use it to send sign-in emails, and may
  use it to tell you about important changes to qot or these policies. Nothing else, and never
  marketing without asking.
- **Your notes**, as the list of changes your devices sync (each note's text, when it was
  created, and whether it was deleted). Deleting a note keeps a marker that it was deleted, so
  that another device doesn't bring it back.
- **Sign-ins**: for each device, a token that keeps it signed in (stored as a one-way hash),
  and for each sign-in started, the email it was for and whether it was approved. These expire
  after 30 days and 15 minutes.

To stop abuse of sign-in emails, the server counts recent sign-in attempts per email address
and per network address (IP). It keeps those counts in memory for an hour and never writes them
down. The server doesn't log your requests or your notes; its error logs, kept by our host for a
limited time, may include your account's id.

## The website

This site counts page views with [Plausible](https://plausible.io/data-policy): which pages are
visited, from which site you came, and your country and kind of device. It sets no cookies,
keeps no personal data and can't follow you across visits or sites; Plausible uses your network
address only to tell visitors apart for a day and doesn't store it. Pages are served by our host,
which handles your network address to deliver them.

The qot apps have no analytics: the apps send nothing to Plausible.

## Who else handles your data

We use a few companies to run qot. Each handles only what its job needs:

- **Railway** hosts the server, its database and this website.
- **Resend** delivers sign-in emails, so it sees your email address and the email.
- **Apple**, if you sign in with Apple: Apple tells us your Apple account's id and email (or a
  Hide My Email address), and we keep a token from Apple only to end that link when you delete
  your account. In the web app, the sign-in screen loads Apple's sign-in script from Apple.
- **Plausible** counts this website's page views (see above).
- **GitHub** and **Homebrew** serve the terminal client's downloads, under their own privacy
  policies.

We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and we would only disclose it if the
law requires us to.

## Keeping it safe

Connections to the server use TLS. Tokens on the server are stored hashed, and the database is
on our host's private network. Every request is checked against your account, and no account
can read another's notes.

## Your choices

- **Export**: `qot export` writes every note as JSON. A signed-in device has everything the
  server has, so the export comes from the device.
- **Delete**: Delete account in the web app's account menu, or `qot delete-account`, deletes your account, your email address, your synced notes
  and your sign-ins from the server, immediately. Notes stay on your devices until you delete
  them.
- **Correct or ask**: email us to ask what we hold about you, or anything else in this policy.

## Children

qot is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly hold anything about them. If you
think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. If a change matters (for example,
anything new the server keeps), we'll email account holders before it takes effect.
